The car is missing. Find the tow first, argue about the bill second.

Impound Ransom organizes official lookup paths and fee research. It is not a law firm, and a rate from one tow type or locality should never be silently treated as the rule for every tow in a state.

How do I find out whether a city towed my car?

Start with the official municipal/NYPD/police/parking lookup or 311 path for the place where the car was parked. Private-property tows can use different contractors and may not appear in a city feed immediately.

Why does the tow authority matter?

A police-ordered tow, parking-enforcement tow, private-property tow, judgment tow and consensual tow can have different rates and procedures. The correct question is not just “What is the state cap?” but “Which rule governs this specific tow?”

Texas private-property tow baseline

Texas TDLR currently lists a statewide maximum private-property tow charge of $272 for vehicles at or below 10,000 pounds unless a lower municipal/county fee applies. Its current fee page lists $22.85 per day for storage of vehicles 25 feet or shorter and a $22.85 impound fee. Verify at the primary source before relying on it: Texas TDLR VSF Fees.

New York City NYPD tow baseline

NYPD currently lists $185 for a regular tow, $370 for heavy duty and $20 overnight storage. Those figures are for the NYPD tow-pound process; marshal/sheriff judgment tows and other programs can use different schedules. Source: NYPD Towed Vehicles.

California is not one magic statewide dollar number

California tow charges depend on the applicable authority and approved/local rate. The CHP publishes current Tow Service Agreements and operator requirements. Impound Ransom labels the framework source separately from any approximate research amount. Source: CHP Rotation Tow Program.

What should I photograph or save?

  • The posted rate card
  • The complete itemized invoice
  • The tow authorization/notice if available
  • The vehicle and property condition
  • Receipts and payment method
  • Any written protest or refusal
  • The official source page and date you checked it

What does “source checked” mean?

It means a primary government source was reviewed for the stated rule or schedule on the displayed date. It does not mean a lawyer reviewed your matter, that a state-wide figure controls every tow, or that the source cannot change tomorrow.
