# Privacy Policy — Impound Ransom

Effective date: 26 August 2026

Impound Ransom helps you locate a towed vehicle and assemble consumer documents. It is not a law firm.

## What we collect
On this client build, plate, city, and last-parked block stay in your browser / on-device storage. Demo purchase flags are stored in `localStorage`.

If you use the Chicago live search, your plate is sent to the City of Chicago open-data portal (`data.cityofchicago.org`) so we can read public tow records.

A production backend (when you connect Stripe or account login) will store:
- plate / city / block you submitted
- purchase records
- optional email for receipts

We do not sell personal information.

## Why
To run the lookup, generate scripts, process payment, and prevent abuse.

## Sharing
- City open-data APIs you invoke
- Payment processors (Stripe, Apple, Google) when IAP is live
- Law-enforcement or regulators only if required by law

## Retention
On-device data remains until you clear site data. Server records (production) are kept for tax and dispute periods, then deleted or anonymized.

## Children
Not directed at children under 13.
