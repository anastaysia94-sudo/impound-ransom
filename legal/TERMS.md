# Terms of Use — Impound Ransom

Impound Ransom is an information and document-assembly product.

1. **No attorney-client relationship.** Nothing in the app is legal advice. Towing and storage rules change. Confirm the posted rate card and current statute.
2. **Official lookups stay free.** Many cities publish find-my-car tools. We charge for organized fee-cap research and generated letters, not for access to public records.
3. **Pay the undisputed amount if storage is running.** Getting the car out usually matters more than winning the argument at the window.
4. **Accuracy.** Live city feeds can lag hours. Private-property tows often never appear on city lists. Always call the number on the lot sign and 311.
5. **Payments.** This repository ships a demo checkout. Production charges are final except where store or card-network rules require a refund.
6. **Acceptable use.** Do not use the product to harass lot employees or to submit false card disputes.
7. **Limitation.** To the extent allowed by law, liability is capped at the amount you paid us in the prior 12 months.
